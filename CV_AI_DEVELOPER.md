## Featured product

**EvidenceDesk · Verifiable Business Data Auditing (2026)**  
Developed a private commercial pilot that checks numerical claims against Excel records, proposes corrected counts and produces reproducible evidence for independent verification. Stack: Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL; public browser demo: HTML, CSS, JavaScript. In the synthetic 200-row example, the browser counts 59 approved records against a claim of 64 and returns ABSTAIN for a missing column. A separate 9,999-data-row example displays archived verified results. The demo uses synthetic data; production readiness and live customer access have not been established.
